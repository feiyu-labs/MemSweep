The cleaning hotkey can now be changed, from Settings → Advanced.

- Click the combination and press the new one; Esc keeps the old one.
- Combinations that need no modifier, that MemSweep already uses, or that another program owns are rejected with the reason shown.
- The previous combination keeps working until the new one is accepted, and the choice survives restart.

---

清理热键可以自己改了，位置在 设置 → 高级设置。

- 点击组合键后直接按下新组合；按 Esc 保留原来的。
- 不带修饰键、与 MemSweep 自身快捷键冲突、已被其他程序占用的组合会被拒绝，并给出原因。
- 新组合被接受之前旧组合一直有效，设置重启后仍然保留。
