Cleaning starts at the level the app recommends, and several things that disagreed with what you saw are now fixed.

- Manual cleaning now uses the Balanced level: it frees idle background apps and low-priority cache, and clears the rest or merges pages only when you choose Deep.
- The right-click menu on a process always frees the app you opened it from, even if memory numbers change while the menu is open.
- If Windows starts MemSweep before the notification area is ready, the tray icon keeps trying instead of staying missing.
- A system-managed pagefile is shown as system managed rather than reported as missing, and the startup switch reflects the real scheduled task.
- The Efficiency mode note fits on one line, and English text is complete sentences throughout.
