Automatic cleaning now reports itself when it actually frees memory, so you can tell it ran without watching the panel.

- A tray notification appears only when a pass reclaims at least 64 MB, at most once every ten minutes.
- Passes that find nothing stay silent.
- The line under the main button says whether the last clean was automatic or one you started.
