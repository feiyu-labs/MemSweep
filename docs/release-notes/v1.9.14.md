MemSweep stays quiet while you are playing: notifications no longer appear over fullscreen games, presentations or Focus Assist, and a window covering the whole screen is enough to hold them back.

- Notification shown only when Windows says you are free to be interrupted; borderless fullscreen games are detected as well.
- Automatic-clean notifications are silent.
- The tray tooltip now shows how long ago the last clean ran.

---

全屏游戏、演示模式或专注助手打开时，MemSweep 不再弹出任何通知；任何窗口占满整个屏幕时同样如此。

- 只在系统认为你可以被打扰时才提示，无边框全屏游戏也能识别。
- 自动清理的通知改为静音。
- 托盘悬浮提示新增「上次清理」时间。
