This release trims redundant diagnostics and internal overhead, making MemSweep smaller and lighter while keeping all Windows 11 cleanup and responsiveness improvements.

- Keep Priority-5 standby clearing, compression-aware cooldowns, background CPU and disk I/O efficiency scheduling, and app-grouped Processes management.
- Remove the background post-clean diagnostic probe and redundant commit reporting, keeping logs and exports focused on what matters.
- Consolidate process tracking and remove duplicate helpers, shrinking the executable below v1.9.20 size.

---

这个版本精简了冗余诊断与内部状态开销，在保留全部核心清理和流畅度优化的前提下进一步缩小了体积。

- 保留 Windows 11 高优先级待机清理、高压缩防回弹冷却、后台效率调度（CPU、I/O 与内存优先级）以及按应用合并的「进程」视图。
- 去掉不进界面的清理后 10 秒复核探针与重复统计输出，让日志和导出诊断更聚焦。
- 合并进程跟踪与文件读写逻辑并清理模板膨胀，把单文件体积进一步压回到比 v1.9.20 更轻的水平。
