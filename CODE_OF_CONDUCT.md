# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the
MemSweep community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
background, identity, or level of experience.

## Our standards

- 使用友善与专业的语言；对事不对人。
- 接受建设性批评，也优雅地提出批评。
- 针对垃圾行为（辱骂、骚扰、人身攻击、发布他人隐私信息）,
  项目维护者有权删除评论、封禁参与者。

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainers via GitHub (issue or private message).
维护者会在 7 天内回应并酌情处理。
