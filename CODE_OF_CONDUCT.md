# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to keep participation in the MemSweep community respectful and free of harassment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Our standards

- Use clear, respectful language and focus on technical issues rather than people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Do not post abuse, harassment, personal attacks, or private information. Maintainers may remove off-topic or abusive comments and ban repeat offenders.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers via GitHub (Security advisories or repository contact), or open an issue when appropriate. Maintainers respond within seven days.

## 中文摘要

欢迎友善、务实地讨论技术问题，对事不对人，尊重不同背景的贡献者。严禁辱骂、骚扰、人身攻击或泄露他人隐私，维护者有权删除违规内容并封禁账号。遇到违规行为可通过 GitHub 联系维护者，七天内回应处理。
